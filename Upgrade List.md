# Description
> Most can be found by exploring the map, and some fragment cant be bought from the shop

## Health Boosts
> Collecting health boost fragment will increase the max level based on the level (the max level is health boost IV)

## Energy Boost
> Collecting health boost fragment will increase the max level based on the level (the max level is health boost IV)

## Energy Regen
> Collecting health boost fragment will increase the max level based on the level (the max level is energy regen II)
