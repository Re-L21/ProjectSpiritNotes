## Mobs
> Standard types of enemy that roams throughout the map
### Ground

1. standard (melee / ranged)
2. Shielded (melee / ranged)
3. crawler (melee)
	- close range attack
	- leap attack
4. aggressive (melee)
5. heavy (melee)
6. ambusher / lurker (melee / ranged)
	- melee will attack player when in range (hidden when out of range)
	- ranged can snipe player across the room

### Aerial

1. standard (ranged)
2. aggressive (melee)
3. heavy (ranged)
	- charge the attack, can summon 3 mini standard enemy (aspid hollow knight)
4. support (ranged)
	- will buff any other enemy

### Stationary 
>this one might be categorized as LDI
1. standard (ranged / melee)
	- melee will attack anyone who is in front of it
2. turret (ranged)
