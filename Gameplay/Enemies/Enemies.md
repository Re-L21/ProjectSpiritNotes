- # Mobs 
	A standard type of enemies ==that can take up to 6 hits (not including the tank/ heavy)== ^098e22
	- ## Ground
		1. standard (melee / ranged)
		2. shielded (melee / ranged)
		3. crawler (melee)
			1. close range attack
			2. leap attack
		4. aggressive (melee)
		5. heavy (melee)
		6. ambusher / lurker (melee / ranged)
			1. melee will attack player when in range (hidden when out of range)
			2. ranged can snipe player across the room
	
	- ## Aerial
		1. standard (ranged)
		2. aggressive (melee)
		3. heavy (ranged)
			- charge the attack, can summon 3 mini standard enemy (aspid hollow knight)
		4. support (ranged)
			- will buff any other enemy
